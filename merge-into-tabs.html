      FAQ

      Is my data uploaded anywhere?

      No. Your browser reads the files and builds the workbook on your own device. StackMySheets has no server that receives files, so your data never leaves the browser tab.

      How are the tabs named?

      A file with one sheet (or a CSV) becomes a tab named after the file, without the extension. Each sheet of a multi-tab file becomes “file name - sheet name”.

      Excel limits tab names to 31 characters and doesn't allow \ / ? * [ ] :, so long names are shortened (the file part first), those characters are replaced with a dash, and repeated names get “ (2)”, “ (3)” and so on.

      Will my dates, numbers and leading zeros survive?

      Yes. Cells are copied with their values and number formats, so dates stay dates, percentages and currency keep their format, and IDs like 00123 keep their zeros. In CSV files, values that look like plain numbers become numbers; anything else, including dates and IDs with leading zeros, is kept exactly as written.

      Are formulas and formatting kept?

      Formulas are replaced by their last calculated values, because a formula that points at another tab or file could break once the tabs are moved into a new workbook. Number formats are kept; fonts, colors and other styling are not.

      Can I combine everything into one sheet instead?

      Yes. Stack sheets stacks the rows from every selected sheet into a single sheet, matching columns by name.
